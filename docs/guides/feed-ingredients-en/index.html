How to read the results

Open an ingredient to view the percentages supplied by Nongsaro. The original name matters: raw, dried, powdered and other preparations are different records. Source names remain in Korean to avoid conflating distinct ingredients in translation.

Blank fields appear as “Not supplied”; an explicit zero remains zero. Moisture differences affect comparisons. Do not assume that equal percentages in differently prepared ingredients mean equal nutrient intake.

What this tool can and cannot answer

It helps identify which composition values the source reports. It does not certify a finished product, check recalls, establish a safe serving amount or determine whether a homemade recipe is complete and balanced. The data does not provide a species-specific safe/unsafe verdict.

For a packaged food, check the full label, intended animal and life stage, feeding purpose and manufacturer information. For a medical diet or a homemade main diet, discuss the whole plan with the veterinarian managing your pet.

Sources and next steps
