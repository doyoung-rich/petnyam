Can parrots eat apples? Check flesh and species

VCA’s budgie feeding guide includes apple among fruit options. This does not set one serving size for every parrot species. Fruit cannot replace a balanced diet.

What should I prepare?

Wash fruit and size pieces for the bird. This page does not establish seed or core safety: remove those parts and discuss the flesh with your avian veterinarian.

What about juice or jam?

Processed products are not equivalent to fresh apple. Check ingredients and your bird’s species and health.
