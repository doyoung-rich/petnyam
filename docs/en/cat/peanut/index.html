ANSWER AND EVIDENCE LIMITS

Can cats eat peanuts? Plain nuts are not peanut butter

We have not established a safe peanut serving size for cats from the sources checked. This is not a recommendation to feed human nut snacks. Plain peanuts and flavored peanut butter are different products.

What does the source support?

VCA advises avoiding salty snacks and peanut butter containing xylitol. That guidance alone neither establishes plain-peanut toxicity nor guarantees safety.

What if my cat already ate it?

Keep the label and record the amount and time. Contact your veterinarian if concerning ingredients or changes in condition are present. Check unfamiliar treats before feeding.
