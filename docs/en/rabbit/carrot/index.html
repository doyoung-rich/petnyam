Can rabbits eat carrots? An occasional treat, not a staple

Carrots alone are not an appropriate rabbit diet. RSPCA recommends hay and grass as the dietary foundation, with small occasional portions of root vegetables such as carrots.

Are carrot tops and roots equivalent?

RSPCA distinguishes leafy carrot tops within varied greens from the root offered as a treat. They have different roles in the diet.

How much should I give?

We do not prescribe one amount for every rabbit. Discuss the existing diet, hay intake, weight and health with your veterinarian; seek advice for changes in eating or droppings.
