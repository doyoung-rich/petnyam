Can rabbits eat coffee?

Do not feed

Do not give coffee to rabbits.

Plain ingredient guidance; processed products need a separate check.
WhyPreparationAvoidAlready eatenSources

Why this recommendation?

Rabbit nutrition guidance identifies caffeine-containing foods as unsafe. Coffee is not a substitute for water.

What to prepare or check

Provide water and prevent access to coffee and grounds.

When not to offer it

Decaffeinated coffee and milk coffee are not recommended rabbit drinks. Report any ingestion to a veterinarian with the product, amount and time.

Already eaten? Start here

An “avoid” recommendation does not mean every accidental bite is poisoning. Ingredients, amount, time and the animal matter.

  1. Stop further access and keep the food or packaging.
  2. Record the species, product, estimated amount and time; say when an amount is unknown.
  3. If eating stops, droppings decrease or stop, or breathing changes, contact a rabbit-experienced veterinarian promptly.

Do not give human medicines or attempt home treatment.
