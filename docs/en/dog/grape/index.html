Can dogs eat grapes? Avoid raisins too

Grapes and raisins can injure a dog’s kidneys. Do not offer them as treats. If ingestion is suspected, contact a veterinarian even if your dog looks well.

Is one grape safe?

Grape composition, size and individual circumstances vary. This page does not establish a safe number. Check breads and cereals for raisins too.

What should I record after ingestion?

Keep the packaging and record your dog’s weight, type eaten, estimated amount and time. Do not induce vomiting at home or wait for symptoms; a veterinarian should decide treatment.
