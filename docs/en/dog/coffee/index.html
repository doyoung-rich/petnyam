Questions specific to this food

Coffee liquid, beans or grounds?

Identify drinks, beans, grounds or coffee-containing desserts. Adding milk or sugar does not remove coffee ingredients. Use the species-specific warning below and provide the product and ingestion time.

What is being added to your dog’s normal diet?

Consider the main food, other treats and any prescribed diet together. Health and product form can limit general guidance. Planning a treat and assessing something already eaten are different tasks.
