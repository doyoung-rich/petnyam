Can dogs eat xylitol? Check sugar-free products

Xylitol is dangerous to dogs and may cause low blood sugar and liver injury. Contact a veterinarian immediately if ingestion is suspected. Sugar-free does not mean safe for a dog.

Which products should I check besides gum?

Some nut butters, sweets, dental products and baked goods contain it. Look for xylitol, birch sugar or wood sugar on the label. Not every sweetener is xylitol.

Should I induce vomiting at home?

Do not attempt it yourself. Keep the label and record weight, estimated amount and time for the veterinarian. FDA notes that signs may be delayed; appearing well does not rule out risk.
