# AdSense content review checklist — 2026-09-21

This is an internal handoff record and is not part of the GitHub Pages `/docs` output.

| Page | Who and what problem it helps | Added value and evidence | Human review before any AdSense review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/guides/food-emergency` | Guardians who suspect accidental ingestion and need to prepare for a veterinary call | A short, actionable record list; cites ASPCA Poison Control and Merck Veterinary Manual | Confirm local emergency-contact wording and veterinary phrasing |
| `/guides/read-a-verdict` | Guardians who need to understand a PetNyam safety label | Defines all four labels and their limits using PetNyam's published classification model | Confirm label definitions whenever the data model changes |
| `/guides/new-food-checklist` | Guardians considering a new food | Separates ingredient, preparation, amount, and individual-health checks; cites Merck | Confirm that no product-specific recommendation has been added |
| `/guides/processed-food-label` | Guardians assessing a packaged human food | Turns FDA labeling guidance into a product-label checklist | Confirm country-specific labels and each product's ingredients |
| `/guides/xylitol-product-check` | Dog guardians who find a sugar-free product | Explains what to record and links directly to FDA emergency guidance | Confirm emergency wording and do not turn it into treatment advice |
| `/guides/main-meal-or-treat` | Dog and cat guardians comparing a staple food and a treat | Explains the FDA distinction between nutritional adequacy and a snack | Confirm any individual feeding plan with a veterinarian |
| `/guides/food-storage-record` | Guardians with a suspected food or treat problem | Provides a traceable product-record list drawn from FDA storage guidance | Confirm local recall and reporting contacts |
| `/guides/changing-foods-safely` | Guardians considering a diet change | Gives a factual record template without prescribing a transition schedule | Confirm prescription-diet and medical cases with a veterinarian |
| `/policy/privacy` | Visitors who need to understand site and advertising data practices | Separates the observed static-site behavior from Google advertising technology and links to Google's notice | Confirm actual hosting logs, regions, consent configuration, and AdSense account settings |

Automated checks performed: static route generation, sitemap validation, required-content checks, and `git diff --check`.
Not a human veterinary, legal, privacy, or AdSense-account review.
